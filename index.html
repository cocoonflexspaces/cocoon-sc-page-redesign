How It Works

From vision to flawless event — five steps.

We've removed every bottleneck between your idea and a perfectly executed event.

01

Tell us your vision

Share your event details — date, vibe, guest count, must-haves, budget range. No brief is too simple or too ambitious.

02

We build your proposal

Your dedicated SuperConcierge specialist curates a tailored vendor lineup — usually within 48 hours — with transparent, all-in pricing.

03

You review & approve

Compare options, swap vendors, refine details. We iterate until every line of the proposal is exactly right for you.

04

We coordinate everything

Contracts, timelines, load-in logistics, dietary restrictions, AV checks — handled entirely by our team. You stay informed without the overhead.

05

You show up & enjoy

A dedicated Casa Manager is on-site from setup through teardown, ensuring every detail is executed without you lifting a finger.
